MusicMix Account Deletion Request

If you would like to delete your MusicMix account and request deletion of the personal data associated with your account, you can submit a deletion request to Noris Solutions.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to the MusicMix support team using the button below. Please include the mobile number or email address associated with your MusicMix account so that we can identify the account.

Request Account Deletion

Information Deleted

After verifying the request, we will process deletion of the MusicMix account and personal information associated with the account, subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

This may include:

  • MusicMix account information
  • Profile information associated with the account
  • Login and authentication information
  • User-created music library information
  • Other personal information associated with the account, where applicable

Information That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained where necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security, or enforce legal agreements.

Where information is retained for these purposes, it will be kept only for the period reasonably necessary for that purpose.

Processing of Your Request

We may need to verify that the deletion request belongs to the account owner before processing the request.

After verification, the deletion request will be processed within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.
