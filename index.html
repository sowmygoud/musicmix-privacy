MusicMix Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Noris Solutions ("we", "us", or "our") operates the MusicMix mobile application and related services.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use MusicMix.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use MusicMix, we may collect information such as:

  • Name or profile information provided by you.
  • Mobile number and/or email address used for your account.
  • Login and authentication information.
  • Music and other content that you choose to upload.
  • Music library, playlists, and related account information.
  • Technical information required to operate and secure the application.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your MusicMix account.
  • Authenticate your account and provide login functionality.
  • Provide music streaming and library features.
  • Store and manage music or content uploaded by you.
  • Maintain and improve the MusicMix application.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Protect the application against unauthorized access, misuse, or security threats.

3. Music and User Content

Music or other content that you voluntarily upload through MusicMix may be stored on our servers or service providers used to operate the application.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary rights or permissions to upload and use any content that you provide through MusicMix.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate, maintain, secure, or provide functionality for the MusicMix service. Such providers are expected to handle information according to applicable requirements and our instructions.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and services.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the MusicMix service, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

7. Account Deletion

If you want to delete your MusicMix account and associated personal information, please use the account deletion option provided within the application, where available, or contact us using the contact information below.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or legal purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

MusicMix is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
